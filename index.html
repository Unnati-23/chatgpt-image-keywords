Start here

From a keyword to an image.

  1. Open ChatGPT and start a chat.
  2. Choose a keyword below and tap Copy prompt. Replace anything in square brackets with your subject or details.
  3. Paste the prompt and send it. To edit a photo, upload it first and describe what you want changed.

These keywords are ordinary descriptions, not built-in slash commands. Paste the full prompt for a clear request. You do not need a plugin to use this guide.

A simple starting prompt

Create an image of a cup of coffee on a wooden table, photographed as a flat lay with soft window light. Keep the scene uncluttered and the textures realistic.

For a reference photo, tell ChatGPT what to preserve as well as what to change.

Use with an uploaded photo

Edit my uploaded photo using [your chosen keyword]. Preserve the person's identity, facial proportions, hairstyle, outfit and all details unrelated to this edit. Change only [the angle, lighting or style you want to change]. Keep the result natural.

Changing the viewpoint may require parts of a scene to be reconstructed. Check the result for changes to faces, objects and details. For teaching images, review every label and fact before sharing.

Build on what works

Combine with a clear purpose.

Start with a subject and one visual choice. Add a second choice if it helps describe the image you want. More instructions can work, but contradictory ones make the request harder to follow.

  1. Angle first. Decide where the camera should be: overhead, at eye level or near the ground.
  2. Choose the light. Window light, golden hour and nighttime flash each suggest a different setting.
  3. Add a medium or era when it matters, such as 35mm film grain or a polaroid look.
  4. Finish with colour. A warm grade or muted palette can bring the scene together. Skip it if the lighting already gives you the mood you want.
An example with two keywords

Create an image of a woman in a tailored navy suit standing outside a modern office, shot from a low angle in golden hour light. Use realistic proportions, natural fabric texture and a calm expression.

Keep the same subject while testing a new keyword. Change one choice per version so you can see which instruction made the difference.
